Machine Maintenance Schedule — Model MX-200

Inspect the hydraulic pump every 500 hours of operation. Replace the air
filter every 1,000 hours. The coolant system holds 12.5 liters of mixture.
Torque all M8 bolts to 40 Nm. Check the drive belt tension monthly with a
gauge calibrated to 62 Niem. Download the full manual at
https://example.com/docs/manual-en.pdf and keep a {machine_id} label on
each unit. Report defects to maintenance@example.com within 48 hours.
